# Research

My research interests lie at the intersection of **artificial intelligence**, **power and energy systems**, **time-series modeling**, and **energy transition analytics**. I aim to develop data-driven and optimization-based methods for reliable, efficient, and low-carbon energy systems.

## AI for Electricity

Modern power systems generate massive amounts of heterogeneous data from smart meters, renewable generation, electric vehicles, electricity markets, and grid operation platforms. My research explores how machine learning and deep learning can improve the modeling, forecasting, and operation of these systems.

Representative topics include:

- EV charging load forecasting;
- renewable energy and wind power forecasting;
- electricity price forecasting;
- fault detection and system monitoring;
- data-driven scheduling and operational decision-making.

## Time-Series Forecasting and Representation Learning

Energy systems involve complex temporal patterns, non-stationarity, uncertainty, and multi-scale dynamics. I am interested in building robust time-series models that combine statistical learning, decomposition methods, deep learning, and optimization.

Representative topics include:

- hybrid forecasting models for energy time series;
- signal decomposition and feature extraction;
- long-horizon time-series forecasting;
- probabilistic forecasting and uncertainty quantification;
- foundation models for time-series and energy applications.

## Optimization and Decision-Making for Energy Systems

Forecasting is only one step toward intelligent energy systems. I am also interested in connecting predictive models with optimization and decision-making frameworks.

Representative topics include:

- microgrid scheduling;
- renewable energy integration;
- electricity market operation;
- energy storage dispatch;
- AI-assisted decision support for grid operation.

## Energy Economics and Climate-Energy Transition

Beyond engineering systems, I am interested in the economic and policy dimensions of the energy transition. My previous experience includes energy industry analysis, green value investment research, and carbon reduction studies.

Representative topics include:

- low-carbon energy planning;
- hydrogen energy industry analysis;
- climate-energy transition research;
- electricity market and energy derivative design;
- policy analysis based on energy and economic data.

## Selected Research Directions

In the future, I hope to work on:

1. **AI-native forecasting frameworks for power and energy systems**;
2. **large models and foundation models for energy time series**;
3. **data-driven optimization for low-carbon power system operation**;
4. **integrated modeling of electricity markets, energy transition, and climate policy**.
