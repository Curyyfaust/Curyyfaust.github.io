# Chenghan Li (李承汉)

I am an incoming Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering at **Tsinghua University Shenzhen International Graduate School**, supervised by Prof. [Ye Guo](https://www.tbsi.edu.cn/2021/1011/c4964a55661/page.htm). I received my B.S. degree in Electrical Engineering from **Zhejiang University** and the **University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign** through the [ZJU-UIUC Institute](https://zjui.intl.zju.edu.cn/), where I was supervised by Prof. [Ruisheng Diao](https://person.zju.edu.cn/H121050#0).

My research focuses on **AI for Electricity**, especially data-driven modeling, forecasting, optimization, and decision-making for modern power and energy systems. I am also interested in energy economics, climate-energy transition, quantitative finance, and interdisciplinary applications of artificial intelligence.

## Research Interests

- **AI for Electricity:** machine learning and deep learning for power system forecasting, operation, and decision support.
- **Power and Energy Systems:** load forecasting, renewable energy forecasting, microgrid scheduling, electricity market analysis, and energy derivatives.
- **Energy Transition and Climate Analytics:** energy planning, carbon reduction, and data-driven policy analysis for low-carbon transition.
- **Interdisciplinary AI Applications:** quantitative finance, traffic flow forecasting, knowledge graphs, and time-series modeling.
