## Microgrid Scheduling Based on Particle Swarm Optimization

**Keywords:** microgrid dispatch, optimization, renewable energy integration, particle swarm optimization  
[[Code](https://gitee.com/curryfaust/microgrid-dispatch)]

This project studies optimal microgrid scheduling under operational constraints. The framework uses particle swarm optimization to coordinate energy resources and improve scheduling performance.

## Electricity Price Prediction Based on LSO-VMD and LSTM

**Keywords:** electricity price forecasting, time-series decomposition, LSTM, optimization  
[[Code](https://gitee.com/curryfaust/optimization-vmd-lstm)]

This project develops a deep learning architecture for electricity price prediction. The method combines signal decomposition, optimization, and recurrent neural networks to improve forecasting accuracy for complex electricity market time series.
